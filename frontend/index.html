The Accuracy Paradox in Imbalanced Fleet PHM

Why 93.08% Accuracy Is Operationally Useless
Because failure is rare (13.45:1 imbalance, only 6.93% positive cases), a naive model that predicts every battery is healthy achieves 93.08% accuracy while missing 100% of critical road failures (277/277 missed).

Our safety-calibrated cutoff (τ = 0.193) sacrifices 3.5% apparent accuracy to achieve 90.25% recall, successfully intercepting 250 out of 277 dangerous failures before road breakdown occurs.
